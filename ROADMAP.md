# Bull Run Escape roadmap

This is the shared list of what the game is becoming. Update it as decisions change.

## Agreed direction

- The quota stays tied to production. Gains and losses change your money, not the quota.
- The Man is never drawn. He appears through his cut and his notices.
- The Man takes his cut of the crops you keep. You keep all the money from what you sell.
- A better cut gets you through the day faster, gets you more from the day, or both.
- Later in the game, a taxman replaces the Man as the adversary.
- Things you buy earn passive income. This is a core mechanic and it stays.
- Prices, gains and losses create risk that carries from day to day.
- Grizz stays an original character. He takes inspiration from expressive simplicity:
  bold shapes and big, readable reactions.
- All money and markets are fictional.
